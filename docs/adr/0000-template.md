# ADR 0000: Decision record template

**Purpose:** Provide the standard structure for recording a non-obvious architecture decision.
**Audience:** Contributors proposing or reviewing a design decision.
**Status:** final
**Last updated (phase):** 0

## Metadata

- **ADR status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces, constraints, or observed problems require a decision?

## Decision

What did we choose, and what boundary does the choice establish?

## Alternatives considered

Which credible alternatives were evaluated, and why were they not selected?

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder? Name follow-up work, migration concerns, and any deliberate limitations.

## Links

Link to the affected architecture or roadmap entry when one exists.
